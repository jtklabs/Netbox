The higher a surface floats, the more opaque it gets. A dialog has to stay readable over content it did not choose.

Intensity

Set once in tokens/glass.css. Past 32px the blur reads as smeared and costs frame time on long tables.

Where glass stops
Surfaces, edges, rules, hovers
Body text and headings — always full opacity
Status pills and badges — solid tints, or the state stops reading
Primary and danger buttons — solid navy, solid crimson
Anything carrying a number someone will act on

Translucency is for the container. Never for the content.
